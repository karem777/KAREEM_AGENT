OpenAI has introduced GPT-6.1 Sol, an upgraded version of GPT-6 Sol. It offers improved performance across various tasks such as coding, professional work, computer use, and scientific research, while being significantly more cost-effective. GPT-6.1 Sol is available to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and developers can access it through the OpenAI API as gpt-6.1-sol. The pricing for GPT-6.1 Sol is $2 per million input tokens, $0.10 per million cached input tokens, and $10 per million output tokens.
